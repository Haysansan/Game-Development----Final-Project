# The Blade: Twin Vengeance

## What's This?
A third-person action game where you slash through a big guy with a blade. Fight, dodge, and combo your way through the levels!

## Features
- one-blade combat with light and heavy attacks
- Dodge mechanics to avoid enemy attacks
- Upgrades coming soon!

## Controls
- **WASD** - Move around
- **Mouse** - Look/Camera
- **Left Click** - Light Attack
- **Hold Left Click** - Heavy Attack
- **Left Shift** - Dodge/Roll
- **Space** - Jump
- **E** - Interact

## How to Run
1. Open the project in Unity
2. Load the main scene from Assets/Final Project
3. Hit Play!
